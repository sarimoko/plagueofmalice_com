The Contagion

Forged in the underground scene of Chico, California, Plague of Malice delivers uncompromising, virulent death metal. Combining blistering technicality, crushing grooves, and dark occult horror aesthetics, the band crafts a relentless live experience.

Anchored in Northern California's DIY metal community, Plague of Malice has shared the stage with regional heavyweights and continues to spread its sonic pestilence across stages throughout the West Coast.
